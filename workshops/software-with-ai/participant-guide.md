# Participant guide

Bring a laptop with a modern browser and access to an AI tool. Work in a group of three or four.

During the workshop:

1. Read the [project brief](../../projects/campus-helpdesk/brief.md).
2. Complete each module worksheet with your group.
3. Keep a record of important AI exchanges and decisions.
4. Test the working flow with the supplied scenarios.
5. Present what you built, what failed and what you would change.

The quality of the reasoning matters as much as the final screen.
