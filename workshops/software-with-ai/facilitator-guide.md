# Facilitator guide

## Before the session

- Read the project and module material.
- Confirm that students can access an AI tool.
- Put students in groups of three or four.
- Keep one tested demonstration ready in case setup fails.

## During the session

- Demonstrate decisions, including changes and failed attempts.
- Ask groups to record why they accepted or rejected an AI suggestion.
- Use checkpoints from the run of show instead of waiting for every group to finish.
- Protect time for testing and explanation.

## After the session

Share this repository, collect the project reviews and record which parts need clearer material.
