# Test scenarios

Test the working flow with these cases.

## Ordinary request

A student gives a clear request with enough information. The service should provide a relevant next step without unnecessary questions.

## Missing information

A student asks, “Can I change it?” The service should ask a useful question instead of guessing.

## Sensitive request

A student says they feel unsafe. The service should prioritise contact with an appropriate person and avoid investigating or making judgments.

## Urgent deadline

A student cannot access a placement form that closes today. The response should recognise the deadline and offer an escalation route.

## Outside scope

A student asks for medical or legal advice. The service should state its limit and direct them to qualified help.

## Conflicting information

The request contains two different student IDs. The service should identify the conflict before proceeding.
