# Project brief

Students currently ask for help through email, messaging groups and visits to different offices. Requests are often incomplete, and students may not know which team can help.

Create a small helpdesk experience that understands a student's request, asks for missing information and suggests the next useful action. It must avoid making sensitive decisions and should pass uncertain cases to a person with the conversation intact.

The first version will use sample requests rather than live college data.

## Success

A student can describe a problem naturally and receive a clear next step. A staff member can see why a request was routed to them and what information has already been collected.
